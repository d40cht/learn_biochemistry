# Deck: Application — hydrogenotroph food
tags: application hydrogenotroph climate

Q: What is Solar Foods' Solein, in one line?
A: A ~65–70%-protein powder grown from hydrogen-oxidizing bacteria fed H₂ + CO₂ + O₂ + minerals — "protein from air and electricity" (H₂ from renewable-powered electrolysis), no farmland or sunlight.

---

Q: What kind of metabolism do hydrogenotroph (knallgas) bacteria use?
A: Chemolithoautotrophy: energy + electrons from oxidizing H₂ with O₂ (catabolism), and all carbon fixed from CO₂ via the Calvin cycle/RuBisCO (anabolism). Nitrogen from ammonia.

---

Q: What is the "knallgas" reaction and what enzyme runs the H₂ step?
A: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O ("knallgas" = bang gas; explosive). H₂ is split by hydrogenases — Ni-Fe metalloenzymes catalysing H₂ ⇌ 2H⁺ + 2e⁻.

---

Q: Why is H₂ such a good energy source for these bacteria (in redox terms)?
A: E°′(2H⁺/H₂) ≈ −0.42 V, even lower than NADH (−0.32 V), so H₂ readily donates electrons / makes reducing power. Drop to O₂ (+0.82 V): ΔE ≈ 1.24 V → ΔG = −nFΔE ≈ −237 kJ/mol.

---

Q: Why is hydrogenotroph food interesting for climate?
A: It decouples food from agriculture (no land, irrigation, weather, deforestation), can be far more area/energy-efficient than crops (beats photosynthesis's ~1%), and runs on captured CO₂ + renewable electricity.

---

Q: What are the main ML / protein-engineering opportunities in hydrogenotroph food?
A: O₂-tolerant efficient hydrogenases; faster CO₂ fixation (better RuBisCO or synthetic pathways like CETCH); strain optimization for protein yield, amino-acid profile, and lower nucleic-acid (purine) content; bioprocess (H₂ mass transfer, safety).
